# Week 2: Initial Assets Setup

**Date:** February 2025 (Week 2)
**Goal:** Gather and organize the first batch of visual and auditory assets to establish the game's theme.

## Asset Acquisition
Initial research was conducted to find high-quality 2D pixel art and sound effects that match the "Samurai" aesthetic.

### 1. Visual Assets (Pixel Art)
The following asset packs were sourced and integrated into the `assets/` directory:
- **Environment:** "Autumn Forest 2D Pixel Art" for the starting levels and "Castle Set" for internal areas.
- **Characters:** A series of "Samurai" themed character sprites (Samurai #2 through #6, Archer, Panda, Wolf, and Demon variants) to provide variety in enemy and NPC design.
- **Bosses:** "Executioner 2D Pixel Art" was selected as the primary visual for early boss encounters.
- **VFX:** Basic particle sheets for hits and death effects.

### 2. Auditory Assets (SFX & Music)
Basic sound effects were gathered to support the movement and combat mechanics prototyped in Week 5.
- **Combat:** Sword swings (cut1-3), blocking sounds (block1-4).
- **Movement:** Footsteps (step/player_footstep), dashing (dash).
- **Feedback:** Shouts, item pickup/drop sounds.
- **Ambiance:** Initial background music (`bg_music.mp3`) for level testing.

## Organization
- All assets are centralized in the `res://assets/` directory.
- Folders are categorized by type (Characters, SFX, Tileset, Trees, etc.) to ensure ease of access during scene composition.
- `.import` files were generated by the Godot editor to handle texture compression and sound import settings.
