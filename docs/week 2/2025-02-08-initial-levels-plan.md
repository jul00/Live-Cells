# Initial Levels & World Layout Plan

**Date:** February 2025 (Week 2)
**Goal:** Define the scope and structure of the game's world through a variety of level sizes and specialized areas.

## Level Types & Distribution
The game will feature three distinct level sizes to provide a mix of tight combat encounters and broader exploration.

| Level Type | Count | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Small** | 3 | Focus on arena-style combat, mini-bosses, or specific narrative beats. |
| **Medium** | 3 | Standard levels with multiple paths, secret areas, and standard enemy density. |
| **Large** | 3 | Extensive zones with complex navigation, environmental hazards, and large-scale set pieces. |

**Total Main Levels:** 9

## Specialized Areas

### 1. Starting Area (The Outskirts)
- **Purpose:** Serve as the player's introduction to the world's atmosphere and lore.
- **Features:** Safe zones for dialogue, basic navigation practice, and the first glimpse of the game's visual style.

### 2. Tutorial Area (Training Grounds)
- **Purpose:** Teach the player the core mechanics of the "Blade of the Samurai."
- **Focus:** 
    - Basic movement and wall climbing.
    - Combat fundamentals (light/heavy attacks, parrying).
    - Resource management (health/stamina/items).
- **Design:** A structured environment with low-risk encounters to build player confidence before entering the first main level.
